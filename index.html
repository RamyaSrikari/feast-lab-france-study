Executive decision

Proceed—with six conditions.

The opportunity is credible, but the viable business is not informal home production or individual parcels from India. It is a narrow, documented range manufactured in a licensed facility, released against specifications and imported by an EU-established operator.

Verified market evidence

France is buying more spice mixtures.
India is not keeping pace.

Compliance reality

Design compliance into the product.

French-language consumer information, an EU-established responsible operator, traceability, food-contact evidence and live border-control checks are launch inputs—not paperwork added after production.

12-month roadmap

Move from evidence to controlled learning.

Each phase ends with a decision gate. The team earns the right to scale.
